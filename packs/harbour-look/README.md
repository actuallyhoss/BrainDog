# Harbour look

Harbour look changes how every screen is drawn, in light and in dark. The house red becomes a deep sea blue-green
wherever it marks a choice, a primary button, a link or a chart, and the grey of the board and its tiles leans cooler
to match. It is one theme and nothing else: it adds no list, no page and no rule, and none of your things change.

To go back to the house look, remove Harbour look from your plugins: every screen is drawn in the house red again, and
nothing else on your record moves. Late and needs-you keep their deeper red and their mark in either look.
