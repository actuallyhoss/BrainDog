# Studies

For someone studying: school, college, a degree or a course taken alone. It keeps your courses, each assignment with its due day, your marks as plain numbers, a reading list and the time you spent studying.

- **Today leads with what is due this week**, then what is late, your average mark and the minutes studied in a week.
- **Marks are numbers from 0 to 100.** Each course card draws a trend of its marks by month, so you see whether a course is going up.
- **Reading list and study sessions** sit on the same page.
- Nothing runs by itself. One automation is written and switched off: it asks whether to plan a study session when a new assignment is added.
- The preview is made of invented examples. Needs nothing else installed.
