# Sharing and access

You can invite people into your BrainDog, give each of them a role over all of it or a level on chosen lists, and change it whenever you like. Everything they add carries their name, and the owner can always see it.

## Roles

A role says what somebody can do across all of your BrainDog. You pick one when you invite them, and change it in Settings, under People & sharing. Every place that gives access uses the same five words, and **Settings › Who can do what** lays them side by side.

```comparison | The five roles
Role | What they can do | What they can't
Owner | Everything, including people, add-ons, settings, Keys, export and deleting this BrainDog | Nothing is kept from an owner
Can decide | See and change everything, answer what needs a decision, and invite people | Open Keys, change add-ons or settings, export, or make an owner
Can edit | See, add and change what is here, and comment | Answer what needs a decision, invite anyone, or open Keys
Can add | Add new things and comment | See what is here, even what they added, unless you also let them view it
Can view | See what you give them | Change, add or delete anything
```

Somebody who **Can decide** is called an Admin where a sentence needs a word for them. They can invite people and change what others can do, but never make an owner, and never give anybody more than they hold themselves.

**Can add** is for somebody who files things into your BrainDog and should see nothing else, such as a bookkeeper dropping in receipts. Given over all of BrainDog, it also leaves out Keys, People and any list kept for owners.

A BrainDog keeps at least one owner, and you cannot change your own role. Another owner can, which is what a second owner is for.

The first time a colleague opens Today, one line says the role they joined with, what it lets them do and what it does not, with a link to Who can do what.

## Who can do what

```comparison | Who can do what
Thing | Who can
See what is here | Owner, Can decide, Can edit, Can view
Add new things | Owner, Can decide, Can edit, Can add
Change and delete things | Owner, Can decide, Can edit
Comment and suggest changes | Owner, Can decide, Can edit, Can add
Answer what needs a decision | Owner, Can decide
Invite people and change what they can do | Owner, and Can decide for anybody but owners
Share a project or a list with someone | Owner, Can decide
Connect their own agents | Everyone, up to their own role
Let their agents act without asking | Only an owner, for their own agents
Add-ons, settings and what runs on its own | Owner
Keys | Owner
Export, backups and deleting this BrainDog | Owner
Make someone an owner, or hand ownership over | Owner
```

## Private rows

A row you made can be kept to yourself: choose **Private to me** in its row menu, and a lock shows on the row, its card and the board. Nobody else reads it, in a list, a figure, a search, an export or a shared link. Only you can share it again.

## Invite somebody

A second person joins your BrainDog on Cloud Plus, with their own sign-in: the one they use for every BrainDog, never a second password for yours.

```steps
Press Invite a person | Give their email address | Settings, under People & sharing
Choose what they can do | One of the five roles over all of BrainDog, each with what it lets them do, or chosen lists at a level each | In the invitation
It is mailed to them | BrainDog emails the invitation at once, naming you and the role. It works once, for 24 hours, and only for that address. **Copy link** is there too, if you would rather send it yourself | Their inbox
They press Join | The mail carries the proof that the address is theirs, so one mail and one press is enough. They can type their name on the page, then add a passkey | On the invitation page
```

The invitation page says who invited them, to which BrainDog, and the role they join with. An invitation that has not been used is listed with what it gives and the day it runs out. **Withdraw** stops it working. Make a new one if it ran out.

A colleague who holds some lists and not all of BrainDog lands on a Today that names their lists. A place they were given nothing in, such as Money, is not in their sidebar at all, and Settings shows only the parts that are theirs. The rest belongs to its owner, who can see everything they add.

## What a colleague sees

```comparison | What each role sees
Role | Sees | Does not see
Can view | Every list, page and figure, read only | Keys, the runs of your agents, your private rows, who is invited
Can edit | The same, and can add and change rows | The same
Can decide | The same, and People & sharing | Keys, your agents' runs, your private rows, and anything held from them
Some lists only | Only the lists named for them, and pages made of those lists | Everything else, its names and its counts
```

Their agents see what they see and nothing more. Every change a colleague or their agent makes is in **Activity** with their name on it. On each colleague's row in People, **What they did** opens Activity for that person alone. The owner also sees who was invited, given a role, stopped or removed, and by whom.

## Hand ownership over

To give your BrainDog to somebody already in it, press **Make owner** on their row in People. A sheet says what changes before anything does: they become an owner, and people, add-ons, settings, Keys, export and deleting it are theirs. You stay on as **Can decide**, so you keep seeing and changing everything and lose only what an owner does. Only they can make you an owner again.

Press **Hand it over** to do it, or **Keep it** to leave things as they are. If you signed in a while ago, BrainDog asks you to confirm it's you first. It happens in one step, and both sides of it are kept in Activity. Only an owner can hand a BrainDog over, and never an agent, whatever its key or switch says.

Handing over moves who runs the BrainDog. Who pays for it does not move with it.

## Working at the same time

When somebody else is on the same page as you, their face shows beside the bell, up to three and then a count, with their names when you point at it. Nobody is told which page you are on unless they can see that page themselves, and a colleague who holds only some lists sees faces only on what was given to them. Nothing about who was here is kept.

A row you have open updates by itself when somebody else changes it. If you are typing in a field at the time, it waits until you are done. Saving a field they did not touch just works. Saving one they changed while you typed keeps your words in the box and says so: save again to put yours in its place, or cancel to keep theirs.

## Mentions and the bell

Write a name with @ in a comment, on a row, a question in Needs you or a decision, to bring somebody in. The bell leads with **For you**: comments that mention you, and rows handed to you. Somebody who cannot see the thing is never told about it.

A colleague with BrainDog on their phone is rung when somebody hands them something, mentions them in a comment, or joins from their invitation. The phone says only that it happened, never what the row says. Each person turns it off in Settings › Notifications, under **People**. It is never rung for their own changes, it keeps to your BrainDog's quiet hours, and it rings at most six times an hour.

## Share something

The Share sheet shares one thing with one person, a group or an email: a project, a list, the rows a view shows, or a single row. Open it from **Share**, then **Share with people**, on a project's page; from **Share this view** on a list; or from **Share something** in Settings, under People & sharing. Only an owner or somebody who Can decide can share, and Share is not shown to anybody else.

```comparison | The Share sheet
Field | What it says
Start from | Accountant, Contractor, Assistant or Reviewer fill the rest for you
With | A name, a group or an email. An email gets an invitation mailed to it, the same as inviting a person
Can | View, Comment, Edit or Manage, each with a line saying what it lets them do
What | Everything in it, one of its lists, or only the rows where a field has a value
Hide | Amounts, or a field you pick
Until | 1 day, 1 week, 30 days, a day you pick, or no end
More | Weekdays 9 to 6, Just once, Their agents too, and Can delete
```

**Can** says what they can do with what you share. **View** reads it. **Comment** reads it and suggests changes that wait for you. **Edit** also adds, changes and deletes rows. **Manage** is Edit plus sharing it on to people already here, inside their own share. These are separate from the roles above, which cover somebody's whole place in your BrainDog.

A share **Until** a day ends at the end of that day, on your BrainDog's clock.

**Amounts start hidden for anybody outside your team.** Money fields are left out, and amounts written in notes and titles are taken out of the text they read too, whether they carry a currency sign or are written in words, like "Budget is 12k" or "240 pounds". Somebody on your team sees amounts, as their role already does. Press **Amounts** under Hide to change it either way.

The **Contractor** start gives somebody one project to work in, with no money: they open the project and its work with every amount left out.

Under the fields, one sentence says the whole share back, with what they would see now, money included or not. Sharing with no end, or letting somebody new change things, asks you to **Confirm it's you** with Face ID or your passkey first. Nothing is shared until that passes.

On a phone the sheet shows With, Can and Until, and **More choices** opens the rest.

## Different on one list

A role over all of BrainDog covers every list, including lists added later. To give somebody a different level on one list, choose **Different on one part**. It replaces their role for that list only, so it can open more or less than the rest. A list's level uses the same words: Can view, Can add or Can edit.

You can narrow it further to some of the rows: **Only rows where** a field has a value, such as only the projects where Client is Acme. **Back to their level** takes the difference away.

Somebody with **No access** over all of BrainDog sees only the lists named for them, and nothing that is added later.

## Stop, remove, erase

```comparison | Taking access away
Action | What happens
Stop | They are shut out at once. Everything they hold is kept for when you let them back in
Let back in | They return with what they held
Remove | They leave your BrainDog. What they wrote stays, with their name on it
Erase a person | Everything one person wrote is removed, from every place it is kept, in one go. It cannot be undone
```

The People list shows when each person last looked, or that they have not opened it yet. Each of these changes is kept in Activity, with who made it.

## Share one page with anybody

For somebody who should see one page and nothing else, press **Share** on the page and make a read-only link. Anybody with it can read that page as it is now, without signing in. They cannot change anything, open any other page, or see the names of your lists. Choose how long it works: 1, 7, 30 or 90 days. Close it early in Settings, under People & sharing, where each link says until when it works and whether it shows amounts. The link is shown once, so copy it when it appears.

**Include money** says whether amounts, bills and money figures go on the link. Left off, they are not on it at all, and amounts written in its notes and titles are hidden too. It starts off on a project's page, where the bills are rarely meant for whoever holds the link, and on everywhere else.

A link to a project's page shows the project itself: where it stands and its next step, each list of work for it with what is done, what happened lately (without names), and its note. On a link with money, a Money tile says what is still to come in and what came in and went out this month. A link never shows private rows, personal lists, your agents' runs, Keys or Learnings, and nothing from any other page.

Only an owner can make a link. A BrainDog can have read-only links switched off by its host. Share then says so, and still offers sharing with people and as a template.

## Agents have levels too

An agent you connect works at a level too: read only, read and propose changes that wait for you, or read and change rows directly. Its name stays on every change it makes. A colleague's agent can never do more than that colleague. Settings › Agents lists your colleagues' agents by person, with when each was last used, so you can see or revoke one. Connecting one is covered in [Agents](/docs/agents/connect).

## Share how you work, without the rows

**Share this setup**, in Settings under Export, packs the way your BrainDog works (its lists, pages and rules) as an add-on somebody else can install. You pick which rows, if any, go with it as examples. Nothing else of yours leaves.

## Who can share

Free and Cloud are for one person. A second person joins on Cloud Plus, and you can add as many as you like at no extra cost. A self-hosted BrainDog takes as many people as you invite.

## Leaving a BrainDog, or closing your account

Your sign-in belongs to you rather than to any one BrainDog, so your name and passkeys are the same in every BrainDog you can open. **Close your account**, in Settings under Identity, removes your passkeys, your sessions and your sign-in email, and takes you out of every BrainDog you belong to. Each one keeps its own history of what you did there, which its owner can erase. The nightly backups hold your account for up to 30 days, and then it is gone from them too.
