# Sharing and access

You can invite people into your BrainDog, give each of them a level over all of it or over chosen lists, and change it whenever you like. Everything they add carries their name, and the owner can always see it.

## Levels

A level says what somebody can do. The owner sets it in Settings, under People & sharing.

```comparison | The five levels
Level | What it means
Owner | Can change everything, and invite people
Decides | Can change rows, and answer questions in Needs you
Edits | Can add and change rows
Adds only | Can add rows, and read nothing
Reads | Can read, cannot change
```

**Adds only** is for somebody who files things into your BrainDog and should see nothing else, such as a bookkeeper dropping in receipts. Such a colleague cannot even read what they added. Given over all of BrainDog, it also leaves out Keys, People and any list kept for owners.

A BrainDog keeps at least one owner, and you cannot change your own level. Another owner can, which is what a second owner is for.

## Teams, roles and private rows

A second person joins your BrainDog on Cloud Plus, with their own sign-in: the one they use for every BrainDog, never a second password for yours. People hold one of four roles over all of it: **Viewer** reads, **Member** reads, writes rows and proposes, **Admin** also settles who is in it, and **Owner** keeps the rest. An Admin can give others only what they can see themselves, and never changes what they hold.

A row you made can be kept to yourself: choose **Private to me** in its row menu, and a lock shows on the row, its card and the board. Nobody else reads it, in a list, a figure, a search or an export. Only you can share it again.

## Invite somebody

```steps
Press Invite a person | Give their email address | Settings, under People & sharing
Choose what it opens | All of your BrainDog at one level, or chosen parts: Projects, Work, Money and the rest, or a list at a time | In the invitation
Send the link | It works once, for 24 hours, and only for the address you sent it to. Nothing is emailed for you: copy it and send it yourself | Your own mail or chat
They accept | New to BrainDog, they prove the address from a mail, then add a passkey | On the invitation page
```

An invitation that has not been accepted is listed with the level it gives and the day it runs out. Make a **New link** if it ran out.

A colleague who holds some lists and not all of BrainDog lands on a Today that names their lists. A place they were given nothing in, such as Money, is not in their sidebar at all. The rest belongs to its owner, who can see everything they add.

## What a colleague sees

```comparison | What each role sees
Role | Sees | Does not see
Viewer | Every list, page and figure, read only | Keys, the runs of your agents, your private rows, who is invited
Member | The same, and can add and change rows | The same
Admin | The same, and People & sharing | Keys, your agents' runs, your private rows, and anything held from them
Some parts | Only the lists named for them, and pages made of those lists | Everything else, its names and its counts
```

Their agents see what they see and nothing more. Every change a colleague or their agent makes is in **Activity** with their name on it, and the owner also sees there who was invited, given a level, stopped or removed, and by whom.

## Share something

The Share sheet shares one thing with one person, a group or an email: a project, a list, the rows a view shows, or a single row. Open it from **Share**, then **Share with people**, on a project's page; from **Share this view** on a list; or from **Share something** in Settings, under People & sharing. Only an owner or an Admin can share.

```comparison | The Share sheet
Field | What it says
Start from | Accountant, Contractor, Assistant or Reviewer fill the rest for you
With | A name, a group or an email. An email waits for that person, and the sheet shows a link to send them, once
Can | View, Comment, Edit or Manage
What | Everything in it, one of its lists, or only the rows where a field has a value
Hide | Amounts, or a field you pick
Until | 1 day, 1 week, 30 days, a date, or no end
More | Weekdays 9 to 6, Just once, Their agents too, and Can delete
```

**Can** says what they can do with what you share. **View** reads it. **Comment** reads it and proposes changes that wait for you. **Edit** also adds, changes and deletes rows. **Manage** is Edit plus sharing it on to others, inside their own share. These are separate from the levels above, which cover somebody's whole place in your BrainDog.

**Amounts start hidden for anybody outside your team.** Money fields are left out, and amounts written in notes are taken out of the text they read too. Somebody on your team sees amounts, as their role already does. Press **Amounts** under Hide to change it either way.

Under the fields, one sentence says the whole share back, with what they would see now, money included or not. Sharing with no end, or letting somebody new change things, asks you to **Confirm it's you** with Face ID or your passkey first. Nothing is shared until that passes.

On a phone the sheet shows With, Can and Until, and **More choices** opens the rest.

## Different on one list

A level over all of BrainDog covers every list, including lists added later. To give somebody a different level on one list, choose **Different on one part**. It replaces their level for that list only, so it can open more or less than the rest.

You can narrow it further to some of the rows: **Only rows where** a field has a value, such as only the projects where Client is Acme. **Back to their level** takes the difference away.

Somebody with **No access** over all of BrainDog sees only the lists named for them, and nothing that is added later.

## Stop, remove, erase

```comparison | Taking access away
Action | What happens
Stop | They are shut out at once. Everything they hold is kept for when you let them back in
Let back in | They return with what they held
Remove | They leave your BrainDog. What they wrote stays, with their name on it
Erase a person | Everything one person wrote is removed, from every place it is kept, in one go. It cannot be undone
```

The People list shows when each person last looked, or that they have not opened it yet. Each of these changes is kept in Activity, with who made it.

## Share one page with anybody

For somebody who should see one page and nothing else, press **Share** on the page and make a read-only link. Anybody with it can read that page as it is now. They cannot change anything, open any other page, or see the names of your lists. Choose how long it works: 1, 7, 30 or 90 days. Close it early in Settings, under People & sharing, where each link says until when it works and whether it shows amounts. The link is shown once, so copy it when it appears. It shows nothing from any other page: no other project, person, list name or count.

**Include money** says whether amounts, bills and money figures go on the link. Left off, they are not on it at all. It starts off on a project's page, where the bills are rarely meant for whoever holds the link, and on everywhere else.

Only an owner can make a link. A BrainDog can have read-only links switched off by its host. Share then says so, and still offers sharing with people and as a template.

## Agents have levels too

An agent you connect works at a level too: read only, read and propose changes that wait for you, or read and change rows directly. Its name stays on every change it makes. Connecting one is covered in [Agents](/docs/agents/connect).

## Share how you work, without the rows

**Share this setup**, in Settings under Export, packs the way your BrainDog works (its lists, pages and rules) as an add-on somebody else can install. You pick which rows, if any, go with it as examples. Nothing else of yours leaves.

## Who can share

Free and Cloud are for one person. A second person joins on Cloud Plus, and you can add as many as you like at no extra cost. A self-hosted BrainDog takes as many people as you invite.

## Leaving a BrainDog, or closing your account

Your sign-in belongs to you rather than to any one BrainDog, so your name and passkeys are the same in every BrainDog you can open. **Close your account**, in Settings under Identity, removes your passkeys, your sessions and your sign-in email, and takes you out of every BrainDog you belong to. Each one keeps its own history of what you did there, which its owner can erase. The nightly backups hold your account for up to 30 days, and then it is gone from them too.
