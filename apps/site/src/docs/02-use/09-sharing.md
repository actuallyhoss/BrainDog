# Sharing and access

You can invite people onto your record, give each of them a level over the whole record or over chosen lists, and change it whenever you like. Everything they add carries their name, and the owner can always see it.

## Levels

A level says what somebody can do. The owner sets it in Settings, under People & sharing.

```comparison | The five levels
Level | What it means
Owner | Can change everything, and invite people
Decides | Can change rows, and answer questions on Decide
Edits | Can add and change rows
Adds only | Can add rows, and read nothing
Reads | Can read, cannot change
```

**Adds only** is for somebody who files things into your record and should see nothing else, such as a bookkeeper dropping in receipts. Such a colleague cannot even read what they added. On the whole record it also leaves out Keys, People and any list kept for owners.

A record keeps at least one owner, and you cannot change your own level. Another owner can, which is what a second owner is for.

## Invite somebody

```steps
Press Invite a person | Give their email address | Settings, under People & sharing
Choose what it opens | The whole record at one level, or chosen lists, each at its own level | In the invitation
Send the link | It works once, for 24 hours, and only for the address you sent it to | By mail
They accept | New to BrainDog, they prove the address from a mail, then add a passkey | On the invitation page
```

An invitation that has not been accepted is listed with the level it gives and the day it runs out. Make a **New link** if it ran out.

A colleague who holds some lists and not the whole record lands on a Home that names their lists. The rest of the record belongs to its owner, who can see everything they add.

## Different on one list

A level on the record covers every list, including lists added later. To give somebody a different level on one list, choose **Different on one list**. It replaces their level for that list only, so it can open more or less than the rest.

You can narrow it further to some of the rows: **Only rows where** a field has a value, such as only the projects where Client is Acme. **Back to their level** takes the difference away.

Somebody with **No access** on the record sees only the lists named for them, and nothing that is added later.

## Stop, remove, erase

```comparison | Taking access away
Action | What happens
Stop | They are shut out at once. Everything they hold is kept for when you let them back in
Let back in | They return with what they held
Remove | They leave the record. What they wrote stays, with their name on it
Erase a person | Everything one person wrote is removed, from every place it is kept, in one go. It cannot be undone
```

The People list shows when each person last looked at the record, or that they have not opened it yet.

## Share one page with anybody

For somebody who should see one page and nothing else, press **Share** on the page and make a read-only link. Anybody with it can read that page as it is now. They cannot change anything, open any other page, or see the names of your lists. Choose how long it works; close it early in Settings, under Links. The link is shown once, so copy it when it appears.

## Agents have levels too

An agent you connect works at a level too: read only, read and propose changes that wait for you, or read and change rows directly. Its name stays on every change it makes. Connecting one is covered in [Agents](/docs/agents/connect).

## Share how you work, without the rows

**Share this setup**, in Settings under Export, packs the way your record works (its lists, pages and rules) as an add-on somebody else can install. You pick which rows, if any, go with it as examples. Nothing else of yours leaves.

## What it costs

A record is priced per record, and colleagues are free. See [pricing](/pricing).

## Leaving a record, or closing your account

Your sign-in belongs to you rather than to any record, so your name and passkeys are the same across every record you hold. **Close your account**, in Settings under Identity, removes your passkeys, your sessions and your sign-in email, and takes you off every record you belong to. Each record keeps its own history of what you did there, which its owner can erase. The nightly backups hold your account for up to 30 days, and then it is gone from them too.
