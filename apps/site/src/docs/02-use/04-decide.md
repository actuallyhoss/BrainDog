# Needs you

Needs you is where every open question in your BrainDog waits for an answer, and where every change somebody proposes waits for your yes. Nothing an agent, Slash or a colleague proposes goes in until it is answered here.

## What arrives in Needs you

- **Questions a rule asks**, such as whether to chase an invoice that is past due, with the options it offers: Chase it, or Not yet.
- **Changes an agent proposes**: rows to add or change, a new list, a page, a rule.
- **What Slash drafts**: a page, a pattern for the inbox, a way to fit BrainDog to how you use it.
- **An automation you drew**, and changes to one. It runs once you accept it.
- **A look a colleague proposes** for everybody, which the owner accepts.
- **Questions an automation asks** with its Ask a person or Suggest in Needs you steps.

## The order

Questions are ranked by the cost of being wrong, never by age. Each one says what deciding late costs and what getting it wrong costs, so the expensive one is first and you can argue with the order. A question you answer stays in its place as one line, so the list does not jump under you.

## Answer a question

The options on a card are the answer. One press picks one, and the reason is asked for after it.

```steps
Pick an option | Press it on the card, or its number key. a picks the suggested one | In Needs you
Say why | Write a sentence somebody can read in six weeks | In the reason box
Record the decision | Ctrl+Enter (⌘Enter on a Mac) sends it. Esc goes back to the list | In the reason box
```

A decision needs a reason, because it is kept for ever and cannot be edited once it is made. A reason that will not mean much in six weeks gets a nudge, and is kept either way. The decision keeps your name as the one who decided.

Open a question (Enter) to see all of it: why it is asked, each option with its reasoning, the suggested one, and what each cost means.

## Not yet

**Defer the question** (d) asks two things: what has to happen first, and a day to wake you on. The question is parked until then, and comes back by itself.

## Answer several at once

Select questions with x. **Answer N with what is suggested** answers them all with one reason for all of them. When none of those selected has a suggested answer, answer each one instead. Group the list by what each question is about to find the ones that belong together.

A proposal that rearranges your BrainDog is always answered on its own, so it is never swept into a batch.

## Accept a change

A proposed change shows what it will do before you accept it. For a page, what will appear, what it reads from, and where it goes in the sidebar. For a new list, its fields and any example rows. For something that reaches outside BrainDog, each service it will call, the key it will use and what it sends. Accepting approves those calls.

```comparison | What you see before you accept
If it | Needs you shows
Adds rows | Each row, and that nothing is added until you accept
Adds a page | The page drawn, what it reads from, and where it sits
Installs an add-on | What it adds, its example rows, and the rows of yours it touches
Calls a service | Which service, with which key, sending what, and how often
Renames something | Each word that changes, and what it will read afterwards
Takes something away | What goes, and that the rows stay
```

If a row the change would touch has changed since it was proposed, nothing is written. The question is read again so you can look at it with the new row.

If only part of a change could be applied, Needs you says what was done and kept, what was not done, and what stopped it. The question opens again, so you can accept once the cause is put right, or reject it.

## Who can answer

The owner answers everything. A colleague who **Can decide** can answer questions and change rows. Changes that arrange BrainDog itself, such as installing an add-on or adding a page, are the owner's to accept. See [Sharing and access](/docs/use/sharing).

## Talk it over

Every question has a thread under it. A colleague can comment there, and writing a name with @ brings that person in: it shows in their bell under For you, and rings their phone if they have BrainDog on it. Somebody who cannot see the question is never told about it. A comment on a decision goes to its question's thread.

## Decisions afterwards

Every decision is in History and in the feed on Today, with the question and its reason. Search finds decided questions too, under Decided.

## Keys in Needs you

```comparison | Keys in Needs you
Key | What it does
j and k | The next or the previous question
Enter | Open it, with every option and its reasoning
1 to 9 | Answer with that option
a | Answer with the suggested option, or accept the change
d | Defer it, with a reason and a wake date
x | Select it, to answer several at once
```
