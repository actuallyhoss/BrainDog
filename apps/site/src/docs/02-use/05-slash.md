# Slash

Slash is the assistant built into BrainDog. Ask it what you spent on food this month and it looks it up and answers with your numbers. Ask it to add a task or change a row and it does, with an undo. Anything with money, keys, sharing or deletes waits for your yes.

## Ask

Press Ctrl+J (⌘J on a Mac), or click **Ask or change anything** in the bar, and type. The bar widens in place and the answer drops down under it. / and Ctrl+K (⌘K) open the same palette for finding and going places.

```steps
Ask in plain words | "What did I spend on food this month?" or "What's late?" | In the bar
Watch it look | Slash says what it is reading as it goes, such as Reading Money | Under the question
Read the answer | Short, with your figures, and each row it names links to that row | As it arrives
Ask a follow-up | "And last month?" carries on the same conversation | In the same field
```

Every figure in an answer comes from your BrainDog. A figure Slash could not find there is taken out, and the answer says so. When nothing in BrainDog answers the question, Slash says that rather than guessing.

Opened with nothing typed, Slash shows what matters now, or a few questions that fit the page you are on. The **Slash** page in the sidebar lists your conversations, newest first, on every device; open one to carry on.

## Ask for a change

Ask Slash to add a task, change a field, add a person, or draft a page or a rule. What you asked for and can be undone happens at once, with a receipt and **Undo** for a few seconds, and it stays in your history. A change that touches money, keys, sharing, deletes or plugins comes back as a card instead: press Enter to do it, or Esc to leave it. A draft page or rule waits in [Needs you](/docs/use/decide) until you accept it. When Slash read text from outside your BrainDog, such as mail or an import, it asks before every change in that conversation.

## What Slash notices

Slash reads how you use BrainDog and says what it noticed, such as a list that keeps growing or a question you keep asking. Under that are its suggestions, each marked as new, waiting in Needs you, or answered. One press puts the new ones in Needs you and changes nothing else. Only the owner can accept them.

Slash also does quieter work in the background: the inbox's patterns, a digest's summary, the question an automation asks it. That work has its own allowance and never spends your questions.

## Questions and the monthly limit

Each question or change you ask Slash for counts as one Slash question, however much reading it takes.

## Your own key

In Settings, under Agents, the owner can give Slash a key from a model provider of their own. Questions on your own key are billed by that provider, so Slash lists them and sets no limit.

## The palette, without Slash

The palette does more than ask. Press / or Ctrl+K (⌘K) and it searches every list, every decision and every page by the words in them, jumps to a page, opens a row by its number, and runs actions. ? shows the keyboard, which is help and not Slash.
