# Slash

Slash is the assistant built into every record. Ask it what you owe this month and it shows the bills; ask it for a page and it drafts one. It reads your record to answer, shows what it read, and nothing it writes goes in until you say yes on Decide.

## Ask

Press / anywhere, or Ctrl+K (⌘K on a Mac), and type. The palette searches as you type. To ask instead, choose **Ask Slash** under what you typed, and Slash reads the record.

```steps
Press / and type a question | "What do I owe this month?" or "Which clients have not paid?" | Anywhere
Choose Ask Slash | Slash searches the record, then reads what it found | In the palette
Read the answer | Each fact links to the row it came from | Under the question
Open what it read | Which lists it read, how many rows, and the conditions it used | Under What Slash read
```

When nothing in the record answers the question, Slash says so rather than guessing.

The **Slash** page in the sidebar is a home for asking: an Ask field at the top, what you asked lately, and each earlier question with a way to ask it again or open what it filed.

## Ask for something to be made

Ask for a page, a list, a rule or a change to how the record looks, and Slash drafts it as a proposal on Decide. It shows what will appear, what it reads from and where it goes, and **From Slash** marks it. Accept it and it is made; reject it and nothing changes. See [Decide](/docs/use/decide).

## What Slash notices

Slash reads how your record is used and says what it noticed, such as a list that keeps growing or a question you keep asking. Under that are its suggestions, each marked as new, waiting on Decide, or answered. One press puts the new ones on Decide and changes nothing else. Accepting them is Decide's, and only the owner's.

Slash also does quieter work in the background, where the host has a model: the inbox's patterns, a digest's summary, the question an automation asks it. That work has its own allowance and never spends your asks.

## Asks and the monthly limit

Each question you ask Slash, or each thing you ask it to draft, is one ask, however much reading it takes. The pricing page calls these credits.

- A new record starts with a limit of 80 asks a month. The [pricing page](/pricing) lists what each plan includes.
- The owner sets the limit in Settings, under Agents. Zero turns Slash off.
- Usage lists your asks by day: what was asked, when, what it cost and a link to what it filed. See [Usage and limits](/docs/use/usage).

When the month's asks are spent, Slash says so, and says when they reset. Two ways on are offered beside the sentence: use your own key, or open Settings.

## Your own key

In Settings, under Agents, the owner can give Slash a key from a model provider of their own. Asks on your own key are billed by that provider, so Slash lists them and sets no limit.

On a self-hosted record, Slash answers only where a model is set up. A host with no model has no Slash, and says so.

## What Slash can and cannot do

```comparison | Slash, and your agents
Question | Slash | Your agents
Where it lives | In every record. Press / | Claude, Codex, Cursor and any MCP client you connect
What it reads | Your record | What the key or sign-in you gave it allows
What it writes | Nothing directly. Every change is a proposal on Decide | Proposals for you to decide, or rows directly, as you allowed
Who pays for it | Your plan's asks, or your own key | Your agent's own provider
```

Agents are covered in [Agents](/docs/agents).

## The palette, without Slash

The palette does more than ask. It searches every list, every decision and every page by the words in them, jumps to a page, opens a row by its number, and runs actions. ? shows the keyboard, which is help and not Slash.
