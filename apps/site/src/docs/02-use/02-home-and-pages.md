# Today and pages

Today is the page your BrainDog opens on. It answers four questions without a filter: what needs you, who is working, what is coming, and what happened while you were not looking. It is a board of widgets you arrange. Every other page is built out of blocks, and you can arrange those too.

## What Today shows

Today is drawn from your starter's own first page where it has one, and from the house one otherwise. Either way it holds the same kinds of thing:

- **What needs you**: questions waiting in Needs you, bills falling due, anything a rule raised. Answer a question or tick a row where it is drawn, without leaving Today.
- **Right now**: the few figures that matter most, such as cash now or open work.
- **Since you were away**: what you, your colleagues, your agents and your rules changed since you last looked. Today counts "since" from when you last left it, not from when you opened it, so nothing slips past while you are reading.

Today keeps itself up to date. When an agent, a rule or another tab changes something, Today reads the change without a reload.

A colleague who holds some of your lists and not all of BrainDog sees a Today that names their lists and opens them in one press. The first time a colleague opens Today, one line says the role they joined with and what it lets them do.

## Arrange Today

Press **Edit Today** at the foot of Today, or press and hold a widget. A bar stands over the board while you arrange it.

```steps
Add a widget | Press Add widget, search or browse the groups, pick one and its size, then Add to Today | In the bar
Move a widget | Drag it to its new place. From the keyboard, Enter picks it up, the arrows move it and Enter drops it | On the board
Change its size | Small, Wide, Tall, Large or Full, from its handle or Shift and the arrows. Not every widget takes every size | On the board
Hide a widget | Remove it, or drag it to Hidden under the board. It keeps its setup, and a press on it in Hidden puts it back | On the board
Keep it | Press Done. Undo and Redo step through what you changed, and Cancel or Esc leaves without keeping anything | In the bar
```

On a laptop the bar has **Laptop** and **Phone**, so you can arrange the phone's board as well, which keeps its own order. **Arrange as a list** shows every widget as a row with Move up, Move down and its size, for anybody who would rather not drag. What needs you and what is late always stay on Today.

**Make your own**, at the end of Add widget, draws a widget from a line such as "my food spending by week". It goes on Today as a draft for you to keep or throw away, and so does a widget an agent adds.

## Handled today

When BrainDog settles something reversible by itself, such as filing a bill it was sure about, it says so on Today, under **Handled today**. Each line says what was done and why, and has an **Undo** that puts it back. The newest five show, with a count of the rest. Nothing irreversible is ever settled this way.

## Pages

A page gathers what you keep an eye on: figures, charts, tables of rows, boards, calendars, notes. Each block reads from your lists, so a page is never out of date and never holds rows of its own. Removing a page removes nothing from your lists.

Pages sit in the sidebar in sections. Some pages are drawn once for each row, such as a page for each project, from one shared layout.

## Arrange a page

Today is arranged with Edit Today, above. Every other page is arranged with **Edit layout**, in the page's ⋯ menu.

```steps
Move a block | Drag it, or use Move up and Move down. On a wide page, move it between the main column and the side rail | In Edit layout
Change its width | Pick a width from its menu, up to the full width of the page | In Edit layout
Add a block | Press Add a block, say what you want to show, then choose how to draw it | In Edit layout
Remove a block | Remove this block takes it off the page. The rows it read stay in their list | In Edit layout
Keep it | Press Done. Until then, nothing is saved | At the top of the page
```

Adding a block asks two things. **Show me** is what you want: say something, show a figure, show a list, show a plan, or show media. **Draw it as** is the kind, each with a line about what it is for:

```comparison | Some of the blocks you can add
Block | What it is for
Figures | Two or three figures across the top
Bar chart | Amounts side by side, biggest first
Parts of a whole | One total cut into its parts
Board | Cards in columns by state
Table of rows | A list, in full
To-do list | Things to do, with a date
Numbered steps | A sequence, numbered
What happened | What agents, rules and you did since you last looked
Note | A paragraph that matters
```

A page drawn once for each row cannot be rearranged from one of those rows, because every row shares its layout. If a page changes while you arrange it, BrainDog asks you to open it again first.

## Put a list on a page

On any list, filter it down to what you want to watch, then choose **Put this on a page** (Alt+B). It becomes a block on a page, reading the same filter, so the page shows those rows as they change.

## Change this

Open a block's menu and choose **Change this**, then type one line about that block: "show it as a calendar", "sort by due", "newest first", "only this month", "hide paid ones", "top 5". The change is written into the page's own source, the same way the block editor writes it, so Undo puts the block back. A line it cannot read is handed to Slash.

## Make a component and the shelf

**Make a component** in the add menu draws something from a line: a list, a shape such as a calendar, board, table, timeline or bars, a time window and a filter, as in "invoices not paid, next 90 days as a calendar". It becomes an ordinary block you can change. **Save to shelf** keeps a component by name, up to 50, and **Your shelf** in the add menu places it on any page. A shelf item holds how to draw, never the rows, so it always shows today's.

## Ask Slash for a page

Press Ctrl+J (⌘J on a Mac) and describe what you want to see. Slash drafts the page and puts it in Needs you, showing what will appear, what each block reads from and where it goes in the sidebar. Nothing is placed until you accept. See [Slash](/docs/use/slash).

## Pages BrainDog suggests

When a list grows, or you keep asking the same question, BrainDog can offer a page for it. It never places one for you. Offers wait in Settings, under Navigation, until you promote one or dismiss it, and **Suggest new pages** there turns the offers off. Pages you have already placed are untouched either way.

## How a page is built

Every page is written in a small text format: Markdown, with fenced blocks for the figures. **How this is built** on a page shows its source, and you can download it as a file. This page of the docs is written in the same format. The [page dialect](/docs/dialect/overview) section describes it.

## Share one page

**Share** on a page makes a read-only link. Anybody with it can read that page as it is now, and nothing else: they cannot change anything or open any other page. You choose how long it works, and whether money goes on it with **Include money**, which starts off on a project's page. Close a link early in Settings, under People & sharing. The link is shown once, so copy it when it appears. A link to a project's page shows where the project stands, its work and what happened lately. See [Sharing](/docs/use/sharing).

## Moving around

- / opens the palette, to search, jump anywhere, or ask Slash.
- g then h goes to Today; g then a digit opens that item of your sidebar.
- On a page, j and k move between blocks, and o opens the list behind one.
- ? shows every key.
