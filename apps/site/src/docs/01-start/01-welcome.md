# Welcome to BrainDog

BrainDog keeps everything you keep track of in one record, arranged around you. You and your AI agents keep it up to date, and every change says who made it.

## What a record holds

A record is yours alone, with its own database. It holds lists of the things you track (bills, clients, tasks, subscriptions, whatever your life or work runs on), the pages you look at them through, and the history of every change: who made it, whether that was you, a colleague, an agent or a rule. Any change can be undone.

You can export the whole record as one file whenever you want, and if you leave, you take it with you.

## Pages you actually check

A page is made of blocks: figures, charts, boards, calendars and tables, each reading from your lists. You can drag them around yourself, or tell Slash what you want to see.

Every page is written in a small text format called the dialect: Markdown, with fenced blocks for the figures. This page is written in it too, so the blocks below are drawn by the same renderer a record uses, and "Open as text" above shows the words that drew them.

```comparison | Who changes your record
Who | How they reach it | What they can do
You | The app, in a browser | Read and change anything you can see
Slash | Press / in any record | Answer questions and build pages, asking before anything it writes goes in
Your agents | MCP, with a key you give them | Read and write what the key allows, asking you first when they want to change something
```

## Slash

Slash is built into every record. Press / and ask. Ask what you owe this month and it shows the bills. Ask for a page and it builds one. Nothing Slash writes goes in until you say yes.

## Your agents

Claude, Codex, Cursor and anything else that speaks MCP can read and write your record with a key you give it. When an agent wants to change something, you get a question first, and its name stays on the change. Agents and scripts use your API keys without ever seeing them.

## Things that arrive on their own

Forward bank texts and receipts by email, point a webhook at your record, or connect Stripe or Mercury. After three of the same kind, Slash suggests how to file them. Say yes, and the rest go in on their own, without using credits.

## Your first hour

```steps
Start a record | Sign up, and your record opens on its Home | In the app
Bring in what you keep | Forward an email, or connect Stripe or Mercury | In the inbox
Connect an agent | Give it a key and the MCP address | From your agent
Ask Slash for a page | Press / and say what you want to see | On any page
```
