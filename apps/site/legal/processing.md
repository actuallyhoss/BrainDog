# Data Processing Addendum

Last updated: 2026-11-01

## The short version

- If you use BrainDog for a business, you decide what goes in your records. We handle it only to run BrainDog for you.
- We keep it confidential, keep it secure, and tell you if something goes wrong.
- We use a short list of companies to run BrainDog, listed below. We tell you before we add one, and you can object.
- You can export, erase and delete your data yourself, at any time.
- When you leave, your data is deleted, and nothing of it remains 30 days later.

## Who this is between

This addendum is between Quartz Apps LLC ("we" or "us") and the business that uses BrainDog ("you").

It is part of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms), and it applies whenever you use BrainDog for a business and we process personal data for you. By accepting the Terms for a business, you accept this addendum too. If you'd like a copy signed by both of us, write to support@brain.dog and we'll sign one.

Words like "personal data", "controller", "processor", "data subject" and "personal data breach" mean what they mean in the GDPR and the UK GDPR. If this addendum and the Terms disagree about personal data, this addendum wins.

## Our roles

- **You are the controller** of the personal data in your records: the people, clients, colleagues and anything else you, your members, your agents and your connections put there.
- **We are your processor.** We process that data only to run BrainDog for you, as the Terms and this addendum describe.
- **For your account and billing** (your users' sign-in details and your payment details), we are a separate controller, and the [Privacy policy](/legal/privacy) applies.

Annex 1 below describes the processing.

## Your instructions

We process your personal data only on your documented instructions. The Terms, this addendum, and what you and your users do in BrainDog (including what your agents do with the access you gave them) are your instructions.

If the law makes us process your data in some other way, we will tell you first, unless the law forbids it. If we think an instruction breaks data protection law, we will tell you.

You are responsible for having a lawful reason to put personal data in your records, and for telling the people it is about.

We don't sell your personal data, share it for advertising, use it to train AI models, or combine it with data from other customers. Each record is its own database, never shared with another customer's.

## Confidentiality

Only the people who run BrainDog for us can reach your data, and only when they need to. Each of them is bound to keep it confidential.

We open a record only to support you, fix a fault, keep BrainDog safe, or follow the law.

## Security

We keep your data secure with the measures in Annex 2. We may improve them over time, but we won't make them weaker in a way that matters.

## Sub-processors

You agree that we may use the sub-processors listed in Annex 3. Each is bound by a written contract that protects your data at least as well as this addendum, and we remain responsible for them.

Before we add or replace a sub-processor, we will email the owners of your records at least 30 days ahead. If you object on reasonable data protection grounds, tell us at support@brain.dog within that time. We will try to find a way forward. If we can't, you may end the Terms for the affected records, and we'll refund any time you've paid for and not used.

Services you choose to connect, such as your own model provider or the services your connections call, are not our sub-processors. You choose them, and your own agreement with each of them applies.

## Helping you with requests

Most requests from the people in your records can be met in BrainDog itself:

- **Access and portability:** export the whole record, at any time, as one file.
- **Correction:** edit the row.
- **Erasure:** an owner can erase a person from a record in Settings. Their details are removed from the live record and its files at once, and from nightly backups 30 days later. Copies an owner took by hand, and the copy taken before a restore, keep them until those copies are replaced or the record is deleted.

If a person asks us directly about your record, we will pass the request to you and not answer it ourselves, unless the law requires us to. If you need more help, ask us, and we will help as far as we reasonably can.

We will also give you the information you reasonably need for a data protection impact assessment, or to consult a regulator, about BrainDog.

## If something goes wrong

If we become aware of a personal data breach affecting your data, we will tell you without undue delay, and within 72 hours. We will tell you what we know: what happened, what data and how many people it touches, what we are doing about it, and who to contact. We will keep you updated as we learn more.

Telling you about a breach is not an admission of fault.

## When you leave

- **At any time**, an owner can export the whole record, and delete it.
- **When a plan ends**, the record goes back to Free if it fits Free: one person on it, and no other free record of theirs. It stays as it was and keeps working. If it doesn't fit, the record becomes read-only and can still be exported. After 90 days it is deleted, unless it is paid for again. We email you before that happens.
- **After deletion**, every other copy is deleted at once, and we keep one sealed final copy for 30 days. Then nothing of the record remains, in backups or anywhere else.

We keep data longer only where the law requires it.

## Audits

Our security page is published, and once the BrainDog software is published as open source, you can check how it works yourself. We will answer your reasonable written questions about how we protect your data, once a year or after a breach. We don't offer on-site audits. If a regulator requires more, we will work with you on it in good faith, at your cost.

## Annex 2: Security measures

- **Isolation.** Every record is its own database. No table holds two customers' records, and no request can address two records.
- **Sign-in.** A one-time emailed link, Google (which only proves the address and never makes an account) or a passkey. No passwords. Opening saved keys, restoring a copy, deleting a record and letting a new app in all need a sign-in within the last 15 minutes. Repeated failed attempts are locked out.
- **Access for agents.** Keys can read, propose or write, and nothing more. No key can open saved keys, erase data, restore or delete. Keys from approved apps expire after 90 days, and we can end every key at once if we must.
- **Saved keys.** Sealed before they are stored, under a key belonging to the record, which is itself sealed under a root key held only by our servers. An optional keychain password takes that root key out of the path for the keys people open; it is used only while a key is being opened and never stored. Keys a scheduled connection uses stay under the root key, so they can run with nobody there. An export leaves the secret values out.
- **Encryption.** Data travels over HTTPS. Data at rest is encrypted by Cloudflare. Nightly backups are sealed to a key kept outside Cloudflare, so a stored copy alone opens nothing.
- **Backups.** Every record copied nightly, with the last 30 kept. Copies an owner takes by hand are kept apart, up to 30. A failed night is recorded and raised.
- **Accountability.** Every change in a record says who or which agent made it.
- **Slash.** Slash has no way to write to a record by itself: its drafts wait for a person to accept them. It never sees saved keys.
- **Logs.** Our own log lines record what kind of request was made and how it ended, and are written not to carry your content or keys.
- **The code.** A published security page and a private way to report weaknesses. The code itself is to be published as open source.

## Annex 3: Sub-processors

**Cloudflare, Inc.** (USA)

- Hosting and running BrainDog, including your scripts (Workers, Durable Objects, Queues): all data in your records.
- File and backup storage (R2): attached files and sealed nightly backups.
- The AI models behind Slash and search by meaning (Workers AI, through AI Gateway): the parts of your record a question, daily check, daily email or filing draft needs, and the text the search index is built from. Each call asks the gateway not to keep prompts or answers.
- Sending email (Email Service): sign-in links, notices, and the daily email and alerts you asked for.
- Receiving email (Email Routing): the email you forward to a record's address.
- Usage counts (Workers Analytics Engine), if turned on: the kind of each request and a scrambled record ID, never content.
- Bot checks at sign-up (Turnstile): browser and connection details.

**Stripe, Inc.** (USA)

- Payments, subscriptions and invoices: your billing contact and payment details, and each paid record's name and web address. Stripe never receives what's in your records.

**Google LLC** (USA)

- Our support inbox (Gmail): the messages you send to support@brain.dog, and anything from your records you choose to put in them.

GitHub hosts BrainDog's source code only, and never receives customer data, so it is not a sub-processor.
