The owner's legal texts, one Markdown file each (`terms.md`, `privacy.md`, `processing.md`; fair use and refunds are sections of the Terms, not texts of their own), approved and in force since 22 October. `/legal/<name>` draws `terms`, `privacy` and `processing` from here once `LAUNCH.legal` is on (`src/lib/launch.ts`), in plain Markdown: `##` and `###` headings, paragraphs and bullet lists. The folder is outside `src` so a company's name never meets the `shell-words` gate.
